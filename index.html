01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

MORE
THAN A MURAL.

As part of Corona's South African summer campaign, I was tasked with bringing a concept to conceptualize and execute a public-facing billboard artwork in the heart of Braamfontein—one of Johannesburg's most creative urban zones.

This wasn't just another mural — it was a 360° design solution that blurred the line between advertising, cultural storytelling, and immersive public art.

ART DIRECTION ILLUSTRATION OOH PUBLIC ART

02 / THE IDEA

NATURE
HITS DIFFERENT
IN THE CITY.

Rooted in Corona's global messaging, “This Is Living” and “Log Off. Lime In.”, the artwork was designed to interrupt the city's hustle and invite passersby into a moment of calm, nature, and expression.

I developed a custom illustration style that echoed Corona's signature texture-rich aesthetic — using grainy gradients, organic contours, and glow to evoke sunset warmth and natural simplicity.

03 / EXECUTION & IMPACT

BUILT TO STAY.

The artwork was built and installed across two adjoining corners in Braamfontein Square—creating a 180° visibility. I worked closely with print vendors, 3D fabricators, and lighting technicians to oversee the full build from file prep to final install.

This project runs for 3+ years, positioning the brand in a culturally significant way, while also elevating township art styles in a global campaign.
